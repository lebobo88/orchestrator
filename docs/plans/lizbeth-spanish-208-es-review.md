# Spanish for lesson tasks: review for Lizbeth

Status: DRAFT, awaiting Lizbeth's approval

## What this is

When a student chooses Español, the lesson task screens will show the Spanish below. Our team wrote this Spanish as a draft. Nothing goes live until you approve it.

How to respond:

- Reply "approved" if everything is fine, or
- List the row numbers you want changed, with your preferred wording.

Words in curly braces, like {n} or {time}, are filled in automatically (a number, a time, a course name). Please keep them exactly as they are, including the braces.

## Questions for Lizbeth

- "Tú" or "usted"? We used "tú", matching the sign-in page.
- "Tu profesora": we assumed students write to you, so the text says "tu profesora". Is that right?
- "Cuestionario" or "prueba" for quiz? We used "cuestionario".
- "Marca guardada" for the lesson mark. We avoided "Marca registrada" because it also means trademark.
- "Comentarios" for teacher feedback. Does that sound right to you?
- "Corregir y volver a enviar" for "Revise and resubmit". Do you prefer something else?
- Number style: we wrote "20 000" (with a space) for twenty thousand. Do you prefer "20.000" or "20,000"?
