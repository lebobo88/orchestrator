# High-stakes research

Provide informational synthesis, not personalized professional direction. State the applicable jurisdiction, population, regulatory context, time sensitivity, uncertainty, and decision limits. Prefer primary laws/regulations, official guidance, peer-reviewed evidence, recognized standards, and qualified institutional sources. Identify conflicts, exceptions, risks, and evidence gaps.

Include this exact line in the report: `Professional review required before action.` Do not imply compliance, safety, diagnosis, legal sufficiency, investment suitability, or a guaranteed outcome. Escalate missing jurisdiction/context as an intake requirement or blocker.
