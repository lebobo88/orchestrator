# Strategic and general research

For deep strategic/general work, write all eleven sections: Executive Summary; Subject Overview & Context; Comprehensive Market Analysis; Competitive Landscape; Deep-Dive Topical Analysis; SWOT Analysis; Strategic Recommendations; Risk Assessment & Mitigation; Implementation Roadmap; Knowledge Gaps & Further Research; Appendices and Source Ledger.

Include the relevant stakeholders, alternatives, incentives, interdependencies, second-order effects, regional/cultural variation, exceptions, contrarian views, resources, failure modes, and measurable validation. Distinguish established facts, expert consensus, emerging evidence, and speculation. Recommendations must answer What, So what, and Now what without claiming authority over user decisions.
