# Editorial quality

Use with `scribe-core` for revisions, summaries, meeting records, and document-quality repairs.

1. Preserve supported meaning and named constraints. Flag ambiguous or unsupported material rather than silently strengthening it.
2. Normalize terminology, heading depth, references, and reader-oriented ordering.
3. Remove em dashes, flattery, apology filler, formulaic wrap-ups, and unsupported certainty while preserving the author's intended tone.
4. Check descriptive link text, heading hierarchy, tables, and code-fence language for accessible Markdown.
